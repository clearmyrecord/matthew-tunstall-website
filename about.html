Beyond the résumé

I see opportunities where others see limitations.

My professional journey has moved between creative strategy and the real work of building. I have sold advertising, developed new media concepts, pitched products, recruited project teams and worked directly on convention and construction floors.

The through line is curiosity: finding something people need, giving it a compelling story, and doing the practical work needed to bring it into the world. That approach has taken me from East Tennessee's startup community to projects and partnerships across the United States, and now to Las Vegas.

I believe the most useful entrepreneurs can speak both languages — the vision behind a business and the day-to-day operations required to deliver it. I enjoy connecting people, building partnerships and turning a rough concept into something tangible.

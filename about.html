Biography

Matthew “Matt” Tunstall

Entrepreneur, product developer and 2013 Knoxville Chamber Young Entrepreneur Award recipient.

From new advertising ideas to new ventures.

Matt Tunstall developed Stall Talk, an advertising concept that brought branded media to portable restrooms and live-event environments. The Knoxville News Sentinel profiled the business in 2011 and 2014.

In 2013, the Knoxville Chamber recognized Tunstall with its Pinnacle Business Awards Young Entrepreneur Award. His work has since expanded across branded event products, painting project coordination, modular structures and legal-technology concepts.

His ventures include Stall Talk, Box Chairs, United Painters Worldwide, Perfect Dream Modular, RecordPathAI and Potty Favor.

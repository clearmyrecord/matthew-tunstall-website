# FLY IN • FLY OUT — Dallas–Fort Worth preview

This is a **static prelaunch flight/ride-planning demo**, not a reservation service. The trip summary remains on the visitor’s device. No backend, payments, dispatch, driver scheduling or customer data transmission is connected.

## Target airports
- Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) — Terminals A–E
- Dallas Love Field (DAL) — main terminal

## Planned service areas
Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Irving, Grapevine, Plano, Frisco and nearby communities; all subject to authorized carrier coverage.

## Launch gates before advertising or taking bookings
1. Clear brand and domain, determine operator / broker / agent legal role in Texas.
2. Confirm DFW Airport Ground Transportation Administration company/vehicle/driver permits and airport rules for each service type (https://www.dfwairport.com/business/opportunities/groundtransportationbusinessoperations/).
3. Confirm Dallas Love Field City of Dallas operating authority/registration and permitted pickup for each vehicle class (https://www.dallas-lovefield.com/passenger-services/ground-transportation/limousines-sedans-and-black-car-services).
4. Vet commercial insurance and carrier operating authority; execute written transport and agency agreements.
5. Integrate secure server-side booking, quote confirmation, payment, notices, carrier dispatch and flight status. Obtain explicit consent for SMS; publish privacy policy and terms.
6. Run end-to-end dispatch, accessibility, cancellation and refunds tests.

Currently this page must remain explicitly labeled **PRELAUNCH** and should not take payment or imply a confirmed booking.

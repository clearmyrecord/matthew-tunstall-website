Questions
Travel details, simplified.
Can I book my trip to and from the airport together?
The concept supports round-trip planning. A reservation is not confirmed until a licensed carrier approves both legs and the payment/dispatch system is live.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
The planned live platform will track flight status and coordinate carrier policies. Flight tracking is not currently connected in this prototype.
Can you handle convention and group arrivals?
Group transportation is part of the proposed service. Vehicle capacity, licensing, and accessible-service arrangements must be confirmed with an authorized partner.
How much will it cost?
Final fares depend on the authorized carrier, route, vehicle type, and required airport charges. A final quote will be available after partner and payment integrations are connected.

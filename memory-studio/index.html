Questions

Good stories deserve good answers.

Can I download Memory Studio today?

A working Windows prototype exists, but the commercial edition is still being prepared and tested. Join the early-access list to ask about availability.

Does Memory Studio delete my original photos?

The prototype's Studio Trash removes items from the documentary timeline, not from the original photo library. The commercial version will be tested to preserve that safety guarantee.

Will my photos be uploaded to the cloud?

Our first-release direction is local-first, with original media remaining on your computer. Any future cloud or AI feature will require clear disclosure and appropriate user control.

Can I use it for a family documentary or client project?

That's the goal. We're designing workflows for personal memoirs, family histories, and professional storytelling. Edition-specific features are still being finalized.

Is AI documentary generation included?

Not yet. AI-assisted interviewing, transcription, and documentary creation are planned enhancements, not features we're selling today.

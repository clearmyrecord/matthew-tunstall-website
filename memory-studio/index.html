☁MEDIA LIBRARY

Local iCloud Photos connection

Work with photos and videos already synced to your Windows PC through iCloud for Windows. Choose when to scan for new files—no automatic startup scan or Apple sign-in through Memory Studio.

▦ORGANIZATION

Chronological timeline & search

Browse a large archive by date and explore the people, places, events, and stories that shaped your life. Locate moments and build the foundation of a documentary.

✎STORYTELLING

Written memories & tags

Add personal context, notes, and tags to media records. Keep track of the stories behind the photos instead of relying on filenames alone.

◉NARRATION

Voice recordings

Record first-person narration and preserve the audio alongside its note metadata. Keep your Voice_Recordings folder with the project when upgrading.

♫MUSIC STUDIO

Soundtrack & sound design

Import music, narration, and sound effects. Arrange up to 12 audio tracks, trim clips, adjust volume, add fades, preview a mix, and export a WAV soundtrack.

Beta export limit: 10 minutes per soundtrack. WAV export does not embed audio into video.
▣DISTRIBUTION

Streaming submission workspace

Prepare a documentary profile, synopsis, genre, runtime, screener link, rights checklist, artwork, and caption information for prospective distributors such as Pluto TV.

Generates an inquiry email and downloadable submission ZIP; it does not publish to Pluto TV automatically.
✦DOCUMENTARY STUDIO

AI-assisted documentary creation

Record guided interview answers, optionally request AI follow-up questions, transcribe selected audio, and generate editable narration scripts with your own OpenAI API key. Arrange up to 12 photos, add captions and a soundtrack, and export a short WebM documentary draft.

Optional AI requires an API key and separate billing. Video export is a still-photo beta, not an automatic feature-film editor or distributor-ready master.
♻SAFE EDITING

Studio Trash

Hide unwanted items from your documentary workspace without deleting original photographs or videos from your synced photo library.

⇩PROJECT SAFETY

Backup & restore

Export and import written project data to protect your work. Preserve voice audio and imported music separately; a notes backup does not contain every media file.

⌁LOCAL-FIRST

Privacy-conscious workflow

Work with your existing Windows folders and local browser-based project data. The prototype does not upload your entire iCloud Photos collection to Memory Studio servers.

Questions

Good stories deserve good answers.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, you can request access to the free Windows development beta by email. No payment or credit card is required. Beta copies are arranged manually, not through an instant download or automated trial activation. Optional AI usage requires your own API key and separate API billing. Apple and Android apps are not yet publicly available.

Can I download Memory Studio today?

A Windows beta includes manual local iCloud Photos scanning plus an updated universal local import workflow for folders, Android transfers, USB and SD cards, alongside timeline and notes, voice recording, Music Studio, Studio Trash, backups, and streaming submission preparation. The commercial edition is still being prepared and tested; contact us about beta access.

Do I need an iCloud account or an iPhone?

No for local imports. The Windows universal-import beta accepts photos and videos from local folders, USB/SD storage, and Android phone transfers. Apple Photos and iCloud Drive are optional routes for Apple users. The Android app is currently source code only, not a downloadable APK.

Can I move a project between Windows, Apple, and Android?

Manual project conversion is in development. A utility can convert between Android ZIP packages and Apple .memoryproject packages, but the Windows and Apple apps do not yet directly open every format, and automatic sync is not available. Keep backups when transferring projects.

Does Memory Studio delete my original photos?

The prototype's Studio Trash removes items from the documentary timeline, not from the original photo library. The commercial version will be tested to preserve that safety guarantee.

Will my photos be uploaded to the cloud?

The Windows prototype can discover files synced locally by iCloud for Windows without signing into Apple through Memory Studio. It does not connect directly to Apple's cloud servers or upload your original photos. Any future cloud or AI feature will require clear disclosure and appropriate user control.

Can I use it for a family documentary or client project?

That's the goal. We're designing workflows for personal memoirs, family histories, and professional storytelling. Edition-specific features are still being finalized.

Can I add soundtracks and prepare streaming submissions?

The Windows beta includes a local Music Studio for mixing audio and a Streaming Distribution workspace that prepares documentary pitches, rights checklists, and inquiry packages for services such as Pluto TV. These are preparation tools, not automatic video publishing or a guaranteed streaming partnership.

Is AI documentary generation included?

The Windows beta now includes a Documentary Studio for guided interviews, AI-assisted follow-up questions, optional audio transcription, and editable AI narration scripts. Those AI actions require a user-supplied OpenAI API key, separate API billing, and explicit consent before sending selected text or audio. You can also assemble selected still photos into a short WebM documentary with captions and an optional soundtrack without an AI key. An experimental AI Director + MP4 development beta also supports editable first cuts and 720p MP4 export using separately installed FFmpeg; Windows end-to-end testing is pending. This is not one-click creation of a finished feature film or streaming-ready master.

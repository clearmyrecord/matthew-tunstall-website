Questions

Good stories deserve good answers.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Request the Windows v37 development beta by email at no charge. Copies are arranged manually; there is no automatic download or paid activation. Optional AI components or external API services may have separate installation or usage requirements.

What changed in v37 Story Picks?

v37 adds three scene-ranking styles, seven story focuses, explainable reasons, potential duplicate warnings, better video previews, chapter hints and explicit individual or bulk approval. Ranking priorities are not predictions of personal importance, and all media stays available for manual review.

Does scene selection require a paid AI model?

No. Quick Scene Ranking works locally with technical image, metadata and repetition rules. Optional on-device BLIP or CLIP models can add visual descriptions or category hints after separate installation. Those labels can be wrong and should be reviewed.

Can I download Memory Studio today?

A Windows v37 development beta can be requested by email; it is not a finished commercial installer. Earlier prototype tools for guided interviews and audio mixing are not all integrated into the v37 interface. Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android releases are still in development.

Do I need an iCloud account or an iPhone?

No. Windows supports user-selected local folders, USB or SD storage, Android file transfers and downloaded iCloud Photos. The app does not sign into iCloud on your behalf.

Will my photos be uploaded to the cloud?

Story Picks works with selected local files and does not automatically upload image or video frames for AI scene analysis. If you use nearest-city lookup, GPS coordinates are sent to an external online geocoding service. Optional older API-assisted features can transmit specific user-selected content after authorization; check their notices before use.

Does Memory Studio edit or delete my original files?

The v37 Story Picks analysis and timeline approvals work with references to your local originals and are designed not to overwrite or delete them. Always keep a separate media and project backup while testing beta software.

Can I transfer projects between Windows, Apple and Android?

A portable project format and manual conversion tools are in development. Directly opening every project across platforms and automatic synchronization are not available yet. Do not rely on an untested conversion as your only backup.

Are the $59, $119 and $249 editions available?

No. My Life, Family Legacy and Creator Studio are proposed one-time launch editions. No payments are accepted through this preview site, and separate feature permissions and license activation are not implemented in the Windows v37 beta.

Can Memory Studio automatically publish to Pluto TV?

No. The Windows beta can assist with movie exports and preparation of rights checklists and submission materials. Acceptance, delivery, rights clearance and publisher or distributor agreements are separate steps; no streaming-platform acceptance is guaranteed.

Can it automatically produce a complete feature-length movie?

Not yet. The Windows beta includes experimental first cuts, chapters, storyboards, narration, music and export workflows. It requires human scene review and editing, suitable source media and separately installed dependencies such as FFmpeg. Full production validation and commercial mastering are still ahead.
